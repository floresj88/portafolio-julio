Julio Flores Salvatierra

Economist, IFC – World Bank Group · Washington, DC

Private Sector Development · Public Policies · Climate Strategy · Macroeconomics

Julio Flores Salvatierra is an economist at the International Finance Corporation (IFC), World Bank Group, where he leads Country Private Sector Diagnostic work across Latin America, Africa and Asia. He previously served as Macroeconomic Policy Director and Principal Advisor on Infrastructure at Peru's Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF), Knowledge Specialist at McKinsey & Company, and Technical Advisor at Proinversión. He holds a Master in Public Administration in International Development (MPA/ID) from Harvard University and a Master in Economic Theory from the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú.
